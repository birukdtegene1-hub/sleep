What happens during a sleep cycle?

Light Sleep

Entry point of every cycle. Heart rate slows, muscles relax — easy to wake without grogginess.

Deep Sleep

Body repairs tissue and strengthens immunity. Heaviest in early cycles — waking here causes grogginess.

REM Sleep

Dreams and emotional processing. REM windows grow longer each cycle — why mornings feel dream-heavy.

Most adults feel best after 4–6 full cycles (6–9 hrs). Waking between cycles rather than mid-cycle feels less groggy — that's the gap Lull lines up for you.
